I am Bingchen Gong (鞏炳辰 | 巩炳辰 | gǒng bǐng chén). I earned my PhD from [HKU](https://www.hku.hk/) under the supervision of [Prof. Yizhou YU](https://www.cs.hku.hk/people/academic-staff/yzyu), then continued my academic career at [CUHK](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), where I worked under the guidance of [Prof. Dou Qi](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/people/faculty/qi-dou/). I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at [LIX](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/), [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/) with [Maks Ovsjanikov](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/~maks/).

## Research

Over the past few years, my concentration has centered around computer vision and graphics, with a primary focus on leveraging deep learning techniques. I am intrigued by the exploration of various representations for visual data, encompassing not only images but also 3D environments. My thesis delved into generative models of point clouds and super-resolution; however, my current endeavors are predominantly geared towards NeRF-related tasks.

For publications, news, and my CV, see my academic portfolio at [s2.hk](https://s2.hk).

## Beyond work

Having had the privilege to live in both Hong Kong and Paris, and to work with outstanding researchers on exciting topics, I feel this is one of the best times of my life. Outside of work, I enjoy hiking and biking, and I am currently learning to ski. I also love exploring nature and experiencing diverse cultures, from Hong Kong and Europe to other parts of the world.

Born in the Internet era, `h4c|<!ng` has always been my passion. I firmly believe in the spirit of free software and aspire for the cyber space to remain open and egalitarian. My expertise encompasses programming across a range of languages, enabling me to tackle diverse tasks spanning operating systems, GPU computation, encryption, and networking.
