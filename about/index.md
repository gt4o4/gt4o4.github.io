I write here as **Wenri JUSHI**. This is a notebook for things I build, run, break, and eventually understand.

## What I write about

Most posts begin with a practical problem: getting a reproducible system onto an awkward machine, keeping an old computer useful, or making a remote deployment recoverable when something goes wrong. The details tend to involve Nix, operating systems, networking, and self-hosted services.

I like understanding why a fix works and recording the failures that led to it. A useful explanation should leave enough detail for someone else to try it, question it, or improve it.

## Free software

Being able to inspect, change, and share software matters to me. Running a service yourself is one way to learn how it works; documenting what you find makes that effort useful beyond a single machine.

The [blog](/) is where those notes live.
