Welcome to my personal website! I'm Bingchen Gong, and this is my corner of the internet.

## What you'll find here

- **[About](/about/)** — my background: a PhD from HKU, then CUHK, and currently postdoctoral research at LIX, École Polytechnique.
- **This blog** — technical writing, mostly about the things I build and run: infrastructure, Nix, graphics, and whatever I've recently had to debug the hard way.
- **[Portfolio](https://s2.hk)** — publications, CV and news live on my academic site at s2.hk.

## Research

Computer vision and graphics, with a focus on deep learning: representations for visual data, from images and video to 3D. My thesis was on generative models of point clouds and super-resolution; these days I work on 3D Gaussian Splatting, the surfaces it can yield, and what vision-language models know about 3D geometry. The [about page](/about/) has the current summary and [s2.hk](https://s2.hk) the publications.

Feel free to explore and reach out.
